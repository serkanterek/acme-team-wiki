## Audience
my-team

## Structure
- What we do — why Acme exists, what it makes, and who it makes it for
- How we work — the rituals, the runbooks and the voice everything is written in
- Key decisions — the decisions the team made, with the reason, so nobody re-litigates them
- Products & gadgets — every gadget in the catalogue, what it is for and how it fails
- Field reports — what happened when a gadget met the road
- Key terms — the words Acme uses that nobody else does

## Retrieval brief
Consult this wiki for anything about Acme Corporation: how we ship a gadget, what we decided and why, the voice we write in, who owns what, and what happened the last time a product met the desert. Start at index.md.

## Excludes
Personal notes, customer contracts and anything about the bird's whereabouts. The bird's whereabouts are never known.
