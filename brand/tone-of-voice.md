Acme writes the way it builds. The Q3 brand brief changed the website voice, and this page is the current rule for anyone writing on Acme's behalf.

## The three rules

- **Confident.** State what a gadget does. Do not hedge. If it fails in a known way, say that too, plainly; see the failure notes on every page in the catalogue, such as the [rocket skates](products/rocket-skates.md).
- **Plain words.** A customer standing on a cliff does not want to read "propulsion solution". Write "rocket skates".
- **No jargon.** House terms exist (the [glossary](glossary/key-terms.md) has them), but they stay inside the company. Outside, use the customer's words.

## What changed in Q3

Before the brief, the website said things like "an integrated ecosystem of capture solutions". It now says what we make and who it is for, in the words used on [What Acme does](about/what-acme-does.md).

## Who to ask

Mia owns this page. Anyone can propose a change; the change goes in here, with the reason, so the next person who asks the wiki gets the new voice and not the old one.
