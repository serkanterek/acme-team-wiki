## Context

The [rocket skates](products/rocket-skates.md) were the first gadget to go through the staging canyon under the new rule ([Deploys pass through staging first](decisions/deploys-pass-staging.md)). The run confirmed what the [field report](field-reports/2026-08-11-canyon-test.md) had already shown: the skates do not stop.

## Decision

Retired from field use on 2026-08-18. The design stays in the catalogue, marked as retired, because customers keep asking and the page saves everyone a conversation.

## Consequences

Propulsion is an open problem again. The next candidate has to pass staging at full speed with a working brake, which no Acme product has done yet.
