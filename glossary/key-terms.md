- **RR.** The bird. Fast, blue, uncatchable to date. Never referred to by any other name in the wiki, in case it reads it.
- **The mesa.** Where the customer lives and where every gadget ends up. "Shipping to the mesa" means shipping to the customer; the runbook for that is [How we deploy](runbooks/deploy.md).
- **The staging canyon.** The test road that every gadget runs before it ships. Same cliffs as the mesa, no customer on the skates.
- **Production.** The mesa, with a customer and a bird present. Nothing goes to production without a field report.
- **The beep.** The sound the bird makes at the exact moment a gadget fails. Considered a test signal; if you hear the beep in staging, stop.
- **The tunnel.** Any painted tunnel. Assume it is painted. The [giant rubber band](products/giant-rubber-band.md) and the skates have both found this out.

The company these words belong to is described in [What Acme does](about/what-acme-does.md).
