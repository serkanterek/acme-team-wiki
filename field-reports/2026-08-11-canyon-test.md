Wiley E. ran the [rocket skates](products/rocket-skates.md) on the canyon road on 2026-08-11. This is the write-up from the test log cited above.

## Setup

Straight road, four kilometres, a bend at the end. Bird sighted at the start line. Skates lit on the pull cord, ignition clean.

## What happened

Seconds one to nine: everything worked. The skates closed the distance faster than any gadget before them. Second ten: the bend. Second eleven: the bird went around the bend and the customer went through the painted tunnel on the rock face, which turned out to be painted.

## What we learned

The skates had never been run at full speed before a customer put them on. Everything in the write-up was knowable in advance and nothing was known. That is the sentence that became [Deploys pass through staging first](decisions/deploys-pass-staging.md) three days later, and the reason [How we deploy](runbooks/deploy.md) now has a step that says write the report the same day.

## Follow-up

The skates were retired on 2026-08-18. The [portable hole](products/portable-hole.md) was tested on the same road a week later under the new rule and passed; the bird, as usual, did not fall in.
