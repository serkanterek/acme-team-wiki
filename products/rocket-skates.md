## What it is

A pair of roller skates with a solid-fuel rocket bolted to each heel. Ignition is by pull cord. Top speed is well above the bird's, which is the whole idea.

## What it is for

Closing the distance on a straight road. On a straight road the skates work. The desert has very few straight roads.

## How it fails

There is no brake. The rockets burn for eleven seconds whether or not the road continues for eleven seconds. Every recorded failure involves a cliff, a painted tunnel, or both; the [canyon test of August 11](field-reports/2026-08-11-canyon-test.md) is the reference case.

## Status

Retired from field use; see [Rocket skates are retired from field use](decisions/rocket-skates-retired.md). Still sold to museums.
