## What it is

A flat black disc, about a metre across, rolled up for transport. Placed on any surface, it becomes a hole in that surface. Picked up, it is a disc again.

## What it is for

Traps. Place it on the road, wait for the bird.

## How it fails

It does not, in staging. In the field the recorded failures are all the same: the bird runs over the hole and the customer falls into it. The hole works exactly as designed; the design assumed the bird would obey the hole. That assumption is the subject of most of our [field reports](field-reports/2026-08-11-canyon-test.md).

## Status

Shipping. Every unit goes through the staging canyon anyway, because the rule in [Deploys pass through staging first](decisions/deploys-pass-staging.md) has no exceptions, and because the one time we skip it will be the time it matters.
