## What it is

A rubber band the size of a small bridge, sold with two anchor stakes and a leather cup. Stretch it between two rocks, load the cup, release.

## What it is for

Launching a customer across a canyon faster than the bird can cross it on foot. The energy figures are excellent.

## How it fails

The band does not know which direction is forward. In staging it has launched the cup, the stakes, one rock and, twice, the customer, in that order. It is on the list for the next staging run under [How we deploy](runbooks/deploy.md); until then it ships with a note that says so.
