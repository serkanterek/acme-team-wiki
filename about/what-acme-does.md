Acme Corporation designs, builds and ships gadgets to a small number of very determined customers in the American Southwest. Every product in the catalogue exists for one reason: to catch a bird known internally as RR. Nobody has caught RR. We keep shipping.

## What we make

The catalogue has three families. Propulsion, which today means the [rocket skates](products/rocket-skates.md). Restraint, which means the [giant rubber band](products/giant-rubber-band.md) and its many recalls. And terrain, which means the [portable hole](products/portable-hole.md), our best seller and our least understood product.

## How we ship

Every gadget goes through the staging canyon before it goes anywhere near a customer. That rule is recent, it was expensive to learn, and it is written down in [How we deploy](runbooks/deploy.md). The people who own each part of that process are listed in [Who owns what](people/who-owns-what.md).

## How we sound

Acme writes the way it builds: confident, plain, no jargon. The voice page is [Tone of voice](brand/tone-of-voice.md). Words that mean something specific here, like the mesa and the beep, are in the [glossary](glossary/key-terms.md).
