This is a sample team wiki, published so you can read what a CommonGround wiki looks like before you make one. Acme Corporation is fictional. Its employees, gadgets and field reports are fictional. The schema is real.

Three things to notice while you read:

- Every page carries a short frontmatter block: a title, a one-line summary, the date it was last updated, and the categories it belongs to. The categories come from the [Wiki charter](wiki-charter.md), which every wiki writes for itself.
- [index.md](index.md) is generated from those blocks. Nobody edits it by hand. Claude starts there, follows the links, and opens only the pages a question needs.
- Pages cite their sources and link to each other in prose. The deploy rule, for example, lives in [Deploys pass through staging first](decisions/deploys-pass-staging.md), cites the thread that decided it, and is linked from the runbook and the field report that caused it.

To see the company the wiki describes, read [What Acme does](about/what-acme-does.md).

To start a wiki of your own, go to [commongroundapp.io](https://commongroundapp.io).
