Acme is three people. Titles are loose; ownership is not.

| Person | Owns | Wiki role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rr | The company, the catalogue, and every decision in [Key decisions](decisions/deploys-pass-staging.md) | admin |
| Wiley E. | Field operations: every test in the staging canyon and every [field report](field-reports/2026-08-11-canyon-test.md) | curator |
| Mia | Design and the website, including [Tone of voice](brand/tone-of-voice.md) | member |

rr records decisions. Wiley E. runs [How we deploy](runbooks/deploy.md) and writes up what happened. Mia asks the wiki how we sound before she writes anything, and gets the same answer Wiley E. would get.

If you are new, the company itself is described in [What Acme does](about/what-acme-does.md).
